Price changes

When a price can change, and when it cannot.

Your written price is the price you pay. These are the only reasons it can be different, and none of them happens without your agreement.

A higher prescription

Some treatments cost more above a set prescription, or with a toric lens for astigmatism. Your written plan already shows this, based on the prescription you send.

A different treatment after your exam

If your scans show another treatment suits your eyes better, your surgeon explains why and you get the new price in writing. You decide.

Extra nights you choose

If you want to stay longer, or a flight is delayed, we extend your hotel at cost and rebook your transfer.

Only if you agree

Nothing is added on the day without your written agreement. If no treatment suits you, your deposit is refunded in full.

Questions

Prices and payment, answered.

How much does laser eye surgery cost in Turkey?

With Eye Health Turkey, laser eye surgery costs from £750 per eye (£1,500 both eyes) for Femto-LASIK, £825 per eye (£1,650) for Trans-PRK and £975 per eye (£1,950) for SMILE Pro. Each price includes the exam, treatment, hotel, transfers and 12 months of planned aftercare.

How much is lens replacement in Turkey?

From £1,450 per eye, or £2,900 for both eyes, with trifocal lenses. Cataract surgery with a premium lens is from £1,600 per eye (£3,200 both eyes). UK private clinics publish £2,750–4,988 per eye for trifocal lens replacement.

Is the price per eye or for both eyes?

We show both. UK clinics publish per eye, so our comparison uses per eye too. Your written plan shows the price per eye and for both eyes. Cross-linking is priced per eye only.

Why are prices lower than in the UK?

Mainly because running costs in Turkey are lower. Your written plan names the hospital, the surgeon and the laser or lens model, so you can compare like for like.

Is anything not included?

Flights, travel insurance, and a companion's meals and extra nights. Everything else in your plan is in the price, and nothing is added without your agreement.

Do I pay Eye Health Turkey anything?

No. The hospital pays us a commission, which is part of the package price. You pay nothing extra to us.

Can I pay in euros?

Yes. Euro prices are set separately, not converted on the day, and your written plan shows your price in the currency you pay in.

What if I need a top-up treatment later?

Our written enhancement and complication policy says who pays and how travel is arranged. You can read it before you book. Enhancement and complication policy
