Questions

Before you get in touch.

How quickly do you reply?

Coordinators reply between 08:00 and 24:00, Istanbul time. If you write overnight, you hear from us from 08:00 Istanbul time.

Can I talk to a surgeon directly?

Yes, before you book. An eye surgeon reviews your records and you talk to them on a video call at least 7 days before you fly. Until then, your coordinator passes your medical questions to the surgeon's team.

Is it safe to send my eye reports?

Your reports go only to your coordinator and the surgeon who reviews them, under the consent you give. If you prefer not to use WhatsApp, use email or the surgeon review form. Our privacy notice explains how your details are handled.

Can I visit your office?

Yes, by appointment. Our office is in Kartal, on the Asian side of Istanbul. Your exam and treatment take place at the partner hospital, not at our office.

I had treatment with you and something feels wrong.

If it is sudden or severe, go to your local emergency services first. Then message your coordinator on WhatsApp, so your surgeon's team can advise and arrange a check.

How do I make a complaint?

Tell your coordinator or email info@eyehealthturkey.com, and we reply in writing. [Complaints procedure and response time to be confirmed by Veon.]
