2. What this notice covers

This notice covers personal data we collect when you:

  • visit this website or use its forms;
  • message us on WhatsApp, email or phone us, or ask for a call back;
  • send us a prescription, eye reports or scans for a surgeon review;
  • have a video call with a surgeon that we arrange;
  • book and take a treatment trip to Istanbul, and during your 12 months of planned aftercare.

It does not cover other websites we link to, or how the partner hospital uses your medical records.

3. What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: your name, country, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, preferred language, and how you would like us to contact you.
  • Health data: glasses or contact lens prescriptions, eye reports, scans and images, what you tell us about your eyes and general health (for example conditions you are treated for, medicines, allergies and previous eye surgery), the surgeon's written plan, and the reports and aftercare notes shared with us after treatment.
  • Travel details: travel dates and flight times, your hotel stay, transfer pick-ups, any help you need getting around, and passport details where a hotel or visa letter needs them.
  • Companion details: the name and contact details of anyone who travels with you, and their passport details where the hotel needs them.
  • Payment details: records of your deposit and payments, and bank details for any refund. [Card payments are handled by [payment provider], so we do not see or store full card numbers; to be confirmed.]
  • Your messages with us: WhatsApp chats, emails, notes of calls, feedback and complaints. [Video calls with your surgeon are not recorded; to be confirmed.]
  • Website and device data: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and your cookie choices. Analytics and marketing data are collected only if you agree. See the cookie policy.

Health data is a special category of personal data under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, and special category personal data (özel nitelikli kişisel veri) under KVKK. We collect only what a surgeon needs to review your case and what we need to plan your trip and aftercare.

Where it comes from. Mostly from you. Also from the partner hospital (your plan, exam results, and treatment and aftercare reports), from an optometrist near you if you have a check there, and from a companion or family member who contacts us for you.

Data about other people. If you send us information about someone else, for example a parent you are helping, please make sure they know and agree. [If the patient is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must contact us and give consent for them; age threshold to be confirmed by the lawyer.]

Self-check questions. The questions on our treatment pages give a general pointer only. Your answers stay in your browser: they are not stored, and they are not sent to us or to any analytics or advertising platform. [To be confirmed at build.]

9. Your rights

You have the rights below. Some depend on the legal basis we use, and some have limits, for example where the law requires us to keep records.

  • Access: ask whether we hold data about you, and get a copy.
  • Correction: ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your data. We delete it unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Restriction: ask us to pause using your data while a question about it is settled.
  • Objection: object to our use of your data based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent: at any time, for health data, cookies or marketing. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew.
  • Portability: get the data you gave us in a common electronic format, or have it sent to another organisation.
  • Complaint: complain to a data protection authority (section 10).

Under KVKK article 11 you can also ask: whether your data is processed and for what purpose; which third parties in Turkey or abroad receive it; that third parties are told about any correction or deletion; to object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis; and for compensation if unlawful processing causes you damage.

How to ask. Write to [privacy@eyehealthturkey.com, to be confirmed] or to our address in section 1. Under KVKK you can also apply in writing, by registered electronic mail (KEP) to [KEP address, to be confirmed], or from the email address you have already used with us. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within one month under the GDPR and UK GDPR (extendable by two months for complex requests, and we tell you if so), and within 30 days under KVKK. We do not charge for requests, unless the law allows a fee, for example for requests that are clearly unfounded or excessive.

10. Complaints

Please tell us first, so we can put it right: write to [privacy@eyehealthturkey.com, to be confirmed]. You can also complain to a data protection authority:

  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.
  • European Union and EEA: the authority in the country where you live or work, or where the issue happened. For example: in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie); in Germany, the data protection authority of your federal state; in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
  • Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu, KVK Kurumu), kvkk.gov.tr. Under KVKK you apply to us first. If we refuse your request, you find our answer inadequate, or we do not answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request.
