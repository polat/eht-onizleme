Who we are

An Istanbul company that plans eye treatment.

Veon Health (Veon Sağlık Turizm ve Danışmanlık A.Ş.) was founded in Istanbul in 2022 by Veysel Gerdan and Onur Akdoğan, who had worked for many years in the pharmaceutical and healthcare sectors. The company coordinates treatment in Turkey for patients from abroad, from the first question to the last check-up at home.

Eye Health Turkey is Veon's eye-care brand. It covers laser eye surgery, lens surgery, cataract surgery and keratoconus treatment, and nothing else. Eye treatment has its own needs: the right scans before anything is decided, a named laser or lens, and checks once you are home. Our process is built around them.

How it works, step by step
Our role

What we do, and what we do not do.

Clear roles protect you. This is where our job starts and where it stops.

We do

  • Send your records to an eye surgeon at a partner hospital, and bring back their written plan
  • Arrange a video call with that surgeon, at least 7 days before you fly
  • Book your hotel and transfers ourselves, as a licensed travel agency (TÜRSAB 14790)
  • Give you one coordinator in your language, at every appointment, with an interpreter if needed
  • Put every cost in writing: treatment, hotel, transfers and interpreting
  • Plan your aftercare and stay in touch for 12 months after you fly home

We do not

  • Examine, diagnose or treat you: your surgeon does, at the hospital
  • Give medical advice: medical questions go to your surgeon's team
  • Decide whether a treatment suits you: only your surgeon can, after a full exam
  • Promise results: every eye heals differently, and your surgeon explains your risks
  • Push you to book: no deadlines and no "last places"
  • Share your details with advertisers
Not sure who to ask? Travel, dates, prices and paperwork: ask your coordinator. Anything about your eyes: your coordinator passes it to your surgeon's team, and the answer comes from them.
How we are paid

The hospital pays us. You do not pay us a fee.

Our income is a commission from the partner hospital. It is part of the package price in your written plan, so you see one price and pay nothing extra to us. Your surgeon, not us, recommends your treatment.

How we work

The same steps for every patient.

Whatever your treatment, these six things do not change.

The full patient guide

Everything in writing

Your plan names the treatment, the alternatives, the price per eye and for both eyes, your nights in Istanbul, and what is and is not included.

Your surgeon on video, 7+ days before

You talk to the surgeon who will treat you at least 7 days before you fly, as the UK Royal College of Ophthalmologists recommends. That leaves time to think.

One coordinator, start to finish

The same person from your first message to your final check, in English, Deutsch, Arabic or Kurdish. An interpreter at the hospital if you need one.

Exam first, then treatment

In Istanbul, a full eye exam comes before anything else. Your surgeon confirms the plan, changes it, or tells you it is not right for you.

Aftercare, planned before you leave

Checks for 12 months, by video and at a partner optometrist near you where available, with your records in English.

No pressure

No deadlines and no "last places". You can stop at any step, and a deposit is only taken when you choose your dates.

How to check any facilitator, including us

  1. Ask for the authorisation number

    Ours, AK-0706, is at the top of every page of this website.

  2. Check the company name matches

    The name on the certificate should match the company behind the website. Ours is Veon Sağlık Turizm ve Danışmanlık A.Ş.

  3. Look it up on the official register

    The Ministry keeps a register of authorised facilitators. [Link to the register, to be confirmed.]

  4. Check the hospital and surgeon too

    A facilitator's licence says nothing about medical care. See our partner hospitals and surgeons.

A rule worth knowing. Turkish rules do not allow authorised hospitals to work with unlicensed facilitators. If a company cannot show a Ministry of Health authorisation, do not send it your medical records or a deposit.

Questions

About us, answered.

Are you a clinic or a hospital?

No. We are a licensed health-tourism facilitator. Your exam and treatment take place at a partner hospital in Istanbul, and an eye surgeon there is responsible for your medical care.

Who is Veon Health?

An Istanbul company, founded in 2022, that coordinates treatment in Turkey for patients from abroad. It holds Ministry of Health authorisation AK-0706 and travel agency licence 14790. Eye Health Turkey is its eye-care brand.

How do you make money?

The partner hospital pays us a commission. It is included in the package price in your written plan. You do not pay us a separate fee.

Will I know which hospital and surgeon treat me?

Yes. Your written plan names them both, and you talk to your surgeon on video at least 7 days before you fly.

How can I check your licence?

Our Ministry of Health authorisation number is AK-0706, held by Veon Sağlık Turizm ve Danışmanlık A.Ş. You can look it up on the Ministry's register of authorised facilitators. The steps are in the licences section above.

Can I visit your office?

Yes, by appointment. Our office is in Kartal, on the Asian side of Istanbul. Your exam and treatment take place at the partner hospital, not at our office.

Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

If it is urgent, go to your local emergency services first. Then message your coordinator, who passes it to your surgeon's team. The written policy explains who treats you, who pays and how travel is arranged.
