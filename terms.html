1. About these terms

These terms explain how Eye Health Turkey plans and coordinates your eye treatment trip to Istanbul, what we are responsible for, and what the partner hospital is responsible for. They apply when you ask us for a surgeon review, when you book a trip with us, and when you use this website.

Your agreement with us is made up of these terms and your written plan (section 5). [If the two differ, the written plan applies; to be confirmed by the lawyer.] Your medical care is covered separately, by the partner hospital's own consent forms and terms.

Nothing in these terms takes away any right you have under the consumer law of the country where you live that cannot be excluded by contract.

3. What we provide

As your facilitator, we:

  • send your prescription, reports and scans to an eye surgeon at the partner hospital for review, with your consent;
  • arrange your written plan and a video call with your surgeon, at least 7 days before you fly;
  • book your hotel for the nights in your plan, and your airport, hotel and hospital transfers [under Veon Health's own TÜRSAB travel agency licence; to be confirmed];
  • give you a coordinator who speaks your language and comes to every appointment, and an interpreter if you need one;
  • suggest flight times that fit your appointments (you book your flights yourself);
  • plan your aftercare checks for 12 months, and stay in touch after you fly home;
  • give you an itemised breakdown of the accommodation, transport, interpreting and coordination costs in your package, as Turkish health tourism rules require.

Our coordinators and interpreters are not medical staff. When you ask a medical question, they pass it to your surgeon and bring the answer back to you.

4. What the hospital provides

The partner hospital ([Partner hospital], Istanbul) and its surgeons provide all medical care: the full eye exam and scans, the decision whether a treatment suits you, the treatment itself, medicines, and the medical side of your aftercare.

  • Your surgeon explains the treatment, its risks and the alternatives. You sign the hospital's consent form, in English, before anything is done.
  • The hospital's own terms and patient rules apply to your medical care. [A copy to be attached to your written plan; to be confirmed.]
  • The hospital is responsible for the medical care it provides. [Under Turkish rules, hospitals must hold complication insurance for surgical procedures performed in an operating theatre; scope and current status to be confirmed by the lawyer.]
  • [Whether your contract for medical care is with the hospital directly, and who invoices the package, to be confirmed by the lawyer and Veon.]

5. Your written plan and price

Before you book, you receive a written plan based on your surgeon's review. It sets out:

  • the treatment proposed, and any alternatives;
  • the price per eye and for both eyes, in [GBP or EUR];
  • what is included and what is not;
  • the number of nights in Istanbul and your appointment dates;
  • the deposit, and the balance you pay in Istanbul.

Included in every package: a full eye exam and scans, your treatment, a hotel for the nights in your plan, airport, hotel and hospital transfers, a coordinator in your language (and an interpreter if needed), medication and an aftercare kit, and 12 months of planned aftercare (by video, and with a partner optometrist where available).

Not included: flights, travel insurance, a companion's meals and extra nights.

Prices on this website are estimates. Your price is set in your written plan [and held for XX days; to be confirmed by Veon]. It is confirmed in Istanbul once your exam confirms the plan. If the exam shows that a different treatment would suit you better, your surgeon explains why and you get a new written price before anything is done. You can say no.

8. Changes and cancellations

If you want to change your dates

Tell your coordinator as early as you can. We move your booking if the surgeon, the hospital and the hotel can offer new dates. [Any cost of moving dates, to be confirmed by Veon.] If you want to cancel, section 6 applies.

If we or the hospital need to change something

  • If your surgeon becomes unavailable, we tell you straight away and offer new dates or, if you agree, another surgeon at the partner hospital. If neither suits you, we refund your deposit in full. [To be confirmed by Veon.]
  • If the hospital cannot treat you on the planned dates, the same applies.
  • [Who pays for changed flights if we or the hospital change your dates, to be confirmed by Veon and reviewed by the lawyer.]

If your surgeon decides not to go ahead

Your surgeon may decide, for medical reasons, not to treat you or to delay treatment. That decision is always the surgeon's. Section 6 explains what happens to your deposit.

Events outside anyone's control

If cancelled flights, strikes, severe weather, public health measures or similar events stop your trip, we help you rebook and work with the hotel and the hospital to keep your costs down. [Refunds in these cases, to be confirmed by the lawyer.]

If you need to stay longer

If your surgeon advises you to stay longer for medical reasons, your coordinator rearranges your hotel and transfers. Our enhancement and complication policy sets out who pays.

9. Your part

  • Give full and accurate information about your eyes, your general health, your medicines and any previous surgery. Your surgeon relies on it.
  • Follow the instructions you are given before treatment, for example taking contact lenses out on the dates you are given.
  • Hold a valid passport and any visa you need, and book flights that fit your plan.
  • Take out travel insurance that covers travel for medical treatment. Standard holiday insurance often does not.
  • Follow your surgeon's aftercare instructions, and attend your planned checks.
  • Pay the balance in Istanbul as set out in your plan.

10. Aftercare

Your package includes 12 months of planned aftercare. For laser treatment, that is usually a final check in Istanbul before you fly, a video call in week 1, a check at month 1 with an optometrist near you or by video, and video calls at months 3 and 12, with your records in English. Your plan gives your own schedule.

We arrange these checks. The medical decisions are your surgeon's. Our enhancement and complication policy explains who treats you and who pays if you need further treatment.
