The full patient guide
  1. On WhatsApp

    A coordinator replies

    They confirm what you sent, ask for anything the surgeon will need, and answer your first questions.

    • 08:00–24:00, Istanbul time
    • English, Deutsch, Arabic or Kurdish
  2. Next

    An eye surgeon reviews your records

    A surgeon at our partner hospital looks at your prescription, reports or scans. If something is missing, your coordinator tells you.

  3. Usually within 24 hours

    A written plan with your price

    The treatment the surgeon suggests and the alternatives, with the price per eye and for both eyes.

    • Nights in Istanbul
    • What is included, and what is not
  4. 7+ days before you fly

    Video call with your surgeon

    Meet the surgeon who would treat you and ask every question, as the UK Royal College of Ophthalmologists recommends.

  5. Your choice

    You decide

    Book dates with a deposit, take more time, or stop. Nothing is booked until you say so.

A review is not a diagnosis. Your surgeon confirms what suits you only after a full eye exam in Istanbul, and may change the plan or advise against treatment. If the exam shows the treatment does not suit you, your surgeon offers an alternative if one suits your eyes. If none does, your deposit is refunded in full.
What to send

A photo of your prescription is enough to start.

Send what you have. The more the surgeon can see, the more precise your plan. None of this has to be ready before you send the form.

  • Your glasses prescription: a clear phone photo is fine
  • Contact lens details, if you wear them: soft or rigid, and for how long
  • Eye reports, scans or letters from your optician or eye doctor, in any language
  • Past eye surgery: what was done, when and where
  • Eye conditions and medicines you know about, including eye drops
  • When you could travel, and for how long
No prescription to hand? Your optician can give you a copy of your latest one. Or tell us what you notice, and your coordinator asks the right questions.

Questions

Surgeon review questions, answered.

Is a surgeon review the same as a consultation?

No. The surgeon looks at your records and suggests the options likely to suit you, with a price. A diagnosis needs a full eye exam, which takes place in Istanbul before any treatment.

Do I have to commit to anything?

No. You get a written plan and a price, and decide afterwards. A deposit is only taken when you choose to book dates.

How long does it take?

A coordinator replies between 08:00 and 24:00, Istanbul time. Your written plan usually follows within 24 hours of the surgeon receiving your records.

What if I do not have a prescription?

Send what you have, or tell us what you notice. Your optician can give you a copy of your latest prescription, and your coordinator tells you exactly what the surgeon needs.

Who reviews my records?

An eye surgeon at our partner hospital in Istanbul. We are the facilitator: we organise your trip and coordinate your care, but we do not give medical advice.

I already have a diagnosis. Can I get a second opinion?

Yes. Choose "A second opinion" in the form and send your reports and scans. How a second opinion works.

What if the surgeon says the treatment is not right for me?

They tell you why, and suggest an alternative if one suits your eyes. Sometimes the honest answer is no treatment, and you hear that too.

Can I talk to someone before I send anything?

Yes. Message a coordinator on WhatsApp, or request a call back at a time that suits you.
