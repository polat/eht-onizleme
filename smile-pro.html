How it works

A keyhole laser. No flap, no blade.

Short-sight happens when the cornea focuses light too strongly. SMILE Pro removes a tiny, precisely shaped piece of tissue so it focuses correctly.

  1. The laser shapes a thin lens inside your cornea

    The ZEISS VisuMax 800 femtosecond laser outlines a disc of tissue, called a lenticule, in under 10 seconds per eye.

  2. Your surgeon removes it through a 2–4 mm opening

    The surface of the eye stays almost untouched. Nothing is lifted or folded back, so there is no flap to heal.

  3. Your cornea's new shape brings the world into focus

    Vision is usually clear enough for everyday life the next morning and keeps sharpening over the following weeks.

Is it for me?

Who SMILE Pro suits, and who it does not.

Your surgeon decides after scanning your corneas. These are the usual limits, so you know where you stand before you send anything.

Usually a good fit if you

  • Are 18 or over and your prescription has been stable for at least a year
  • Are short-sighted up to −10.00, with or without astigmatism up to −5.00
  • Play contact sports, or work somewhere a flap could be knocked
  • Have healthy corneas of normal thickness, checked on your scans
  • Suffer from mild dry eyes: SMILE Pro usually affects them less than LASIK

Something else may suit you better if you

  • Are long-sighted: Femto-LASIK or lens replacement is more common
  • Have a prescription above −10.00 or thin corneas: EVO ICL is often the answer
  • Are over 45 and need reading glasses: laser does not fix reading vision, lens replacement can
  • Have keratoconus or early signs of it: cross-linking comes first
  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have an untreated eye condition: wait, or talk to your surgeon

Risks

Risks and side effects, explained plainly.

Every eye operation carries some risk. Your surgeon goes through yours in writing, in your language, before you book.

Common, temporary

Dry eyes

Usually settles within months with lubricating drops. Typically milder than after LASIK, because fewer corneal nerves are cut.

Common, temporary

Halos and glare at night

Rings around lights are normal at first and usually fade over 3–6 months.

Common, temporary

Slightly hazy first days

Sharpness can take a few days longer to arrive than after LASIK. It catches up.

Uncommon

Under- or over-correction

A few people need a small top-up treatment, usually done with surface laser after 3 months.

Rare

Infection or inflammation

Treated with drops when caught early, which is why the next-day check matters.

Very rare

Weakening of the cornea

Called ectasia. Your scans before treatment screen for the risk, and you are not treated if it is raised.

Your surgeon will tell you if your exam shows a higher risk, even if it means no treatment.

Questions

SMILE Pro questions, answered.

How much does SMILE Pro cost in Turkey?

With Eye Health Turkey, SMILE Pro costs from £975 per eye, or £1,950 for both eyes. That includes your exam, treatment, two hotel nights, transfers and aftercare checks. UK clinics publish SMILE prices of £2,695 to £3,750 per eye.

Is SMILE Pro better than LASIK?

Neither is better for everyone. SMILE Pro avoids a flap, so it suits contact sports and usually causes less dryness. Femto-LASIK also treats long-sight and can be sharper in the first day or two. Your surgeon recommends one after your scans.

Does SMILE Pro hurt?

Numbing drops are used, so most people feel pressure rather than pain for a few seconds. Afterwards your eyes feel scratchy and watery for a few hours.

How long do the results last?

The correction is permanent. Your eyes still age, though: most people need reading glasses from their mid-forties, whether or not they had laser.

When can I fly home?

Usually on day 3, the day after treatment, once your surgeon has checked your eyes. Flying does not affect healing.

When can I go back to work?

Most people return to desk work after 2–3 days. Jobs with dust, water or physical contact may need a week or two.

Can I have SMILE Pro if I have had LASIK before?

Usually not, but other options may suit you. Send your old surgery details and your surgeon will advise.

What is the VisuMax 800?

The laser made by ZEISS for SMILE Pro. It creates the lenticule in under 10 seconds per eye. Your written plan names the laser and hospital.

Is it worth going abroad for SMILE Pro?

It can be, if you compare like for like: the same laser, an experienced surgeon, and proper checks once you are home. Add flights and time off to our price, and check who looks after you if you need help later.
