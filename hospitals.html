How we choose

How we choose partner hospitals.

A hospital has to meet all six before we work with it, and we ask to see the documents.

Licensed by the Ministry of Health

A hospital licensed to operate in Turkey, with its own eye department and operating theatres for eye surgery.

Authorised for health tourism

Turkey requires hospitals that treat international patients to hold a health tourism authorisation. They may only work with licensed facilitators.

TÜSKA accreditation by the end of 2026

Under Turkey's 2025 health tourism regulation, hospitals treating international patients must hold TÜSKA accreditation, Turkey's national healthcare accreditation, by 31 December 2026. Each partner's status: [to be confirmed].

Insured for surgery

Complication insurance for operations, which Turkish rules require hospitals to hold, and malpractice insurance for the surgeons. We ask to see both.

Eye specialist teams

Ophthalmic surgeons, optometrists and nurses who work in eye care every day, and the scans your treatment needs, such as corneal topography, OCT and lens measurements.

Ready for patients from abroad

Consent forms and reports in English, interpreters when needed, and a team that can follow up with you by video once you are home.

Questions

Questions about our partner hospitals.

Which hospital will I be treated at?

Your written plan names it before you book. Your exam, treatment and check-up take place at that hospital, with the surgeon named in your plan.

Are your partner hospitals accredited?

Accreditation is shown for each hospital once confirmed, with a way to check it. Under Turkey's 2025 health tourism regulation, hospitals treating international patients must hold TÜSKA accreditation by 31 December 2026.

How far is the hospital from the airport?

[XX] minutes from Istanbul Airport. Transfers between the airport, your hotel and the hospital are included in your package.

Is the hospital insured?

Turkish rules require hospitals to hold complication insurance for operations. We ask our partners for proof of it, and of their surgeons' malpractice insurance.

How do I pay?

A deposit holds your dates. The rest is paid in Istanbul after your exam confirms the plan, by card or bank transfer. Your written plan says who each payment goes to. [To be confirmed by Veon.]

What if I need more treatment later?

The written policy explains who treats you, who pays and how travel is arranged. You can read it before you book.
