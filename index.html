Questions

Before you ask.

How much does laser eye surgery cost in Turkey?

With Eye Health Turkey, laser eye surgery for both eyes starts from £1,500, including your exam, treatment, hotel, transfers and 12 months of aftercare. UK private clinics typically charge £1,295 to £3,750 per eye.

How many days do I need to stay in Istanbul?

Two to three nights for SMILE Pro and LASIK, four to five for ICL and Trans-PRK, and four to six for lens replacement or cataract surgery.

Can I fly home after laser eye surgery?

Yes, usually the day after your check-up. Your surgeon confirms you are fit to fly before you leave.

What happens if I am not suitable when I arrive?

Your surgeon will recommend an alternative treatment if one suits your eyes. If none does, your deposit is refunded in full.

What if I have a problem after I get home?

Message your coordinator on WhatsApp. Your surgeon's team reviews it, and we arrange a check with a partner optometrist near you where available.

Who performs the surgery?

A licensed ophthalmic surgeon at an accredited partner hospital in Istanbul. You see their name and qualifications before you book.
