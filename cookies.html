1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar tools, such as local storage, pixels and tags, work in much the same way, so this policy calls them all "cookies".

Some cookies are needed for the website to work. Others tell us how the website is used, or whether our adverts lead to enquiries. Cookies set by this website are first-party cookies. Cookies set by another company, such as Google, are third-party cookies, and that company's own policy also applies to them.

2. How we use them

  • On your first visit, a banner asks for your choice. Accept all, Reject all and Settings are on the first screen and carry equal weight.
  • Until you choose, only strictly necessary cookies are set. Google tags run in a consent mode that sets no analytics or advertising cookies until you agree. [Google Consent Mode set-up to be confirmed at build.]
  • You can change your choice at any time (section 7).
  • We never send health information, the treatments you looked at or your self-check answers to analytics or advertising providers.

Personal data collected through cookies is handled as our privacy notice explains.

3. Strictly necessary

These cookies make the website work. They remember your cookie choice and protect our forms from spam. They do not need your consent, so you cannot switch them off in the banner. You can block them in your browser, but parts of the website, such as the forms, may then stop working.

7. Your choices

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time, and it is as easy to withdraw as it was to give.

[This button opens the cookie settings panel once the consent tool is installed. A "Cookie settings" link will also sit in the footer of every page.]

  • In your browser: you can delete cookies and block them in your browser settings. If you block all cookies, parts of the website, such as our forms, may not work.
  • When you withdraw consent: no new analytics or marketing cookies are set. We remove the ones we set where your browser allows, and you can clear the rest in your browser.
  • When we ask again: we keep your choice for [12 months], then ask again. We also ask again if we add a new cookie that needs your consent.

8. The rules we follow

We follow the cookie rules of the countries our visitors come from, and of Turkey. [To be reviewed by the lawyer.]

  • United Kingdom: the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and the UK GDPR.
  • European Union: the ePrivacy rules as they apply in your country, for example Ireland's ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. No. 336 of 2011), section 25 of Germany's TDDDG and article 11.7a of the Dutch Telecommunications Act, together with the GDPR.
  • Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the Personal Data Protection Authority's guidance on cookies.

9. Changes and contact

We update this policy after each cookie scan, and whenever we add or remove a tool that sets cookies. The date at the top shows the current version.

Questions about cookies: write to [privacy@eyehealthturkey.com, to be confirmed] or info@eyehealthturkey.com. For everything about your personal data, read our privacy notice.
